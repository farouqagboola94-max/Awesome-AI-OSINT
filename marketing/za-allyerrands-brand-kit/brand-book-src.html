Color

✓ Signal Green owns identityLogo dot, accents, icons. If a passerby sees one color, it's this.

04

Ally — the buddy double

Ally is the second you. While you're at your desk, Ally is in the queue, at the market, at the pharmacy — always leaning forward, always carrying the lime package.

Use Ally for warmth: onboarding, stickers, delivery confirmations, empty states. Never use Ally to deliver bad news, and never redraw the visor or swap the package color — the lean, the visor, and the lime box are the identity.

05

Patterns & textures

Patterns sit at low contrast behind content — texture, never noise. One pattern per composition.

06

Voice

Sharp sharp. No stories.Short sentences. Full stops. Lagos-fluent confidence — "No wahala", "Consider it done."
Sell time, not delivery.Every line answers one question: what do you get back? Hours. Weekends. Peace of mind.
One CTA, everywhere.The signature call to action never varies:
DM OR WHATSAPP TO PLACE YOUR ORDER
