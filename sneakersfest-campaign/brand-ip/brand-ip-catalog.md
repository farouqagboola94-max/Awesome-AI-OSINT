# SneakersFest — Brand IP Catalog (50 Assets)

The complete intellectual-property system for the SneakersFest brand. Every asset below is drafted
visually in `brand-ip-book.html` (10-slide brand book, 1920×1080, all assets drawn as vector SVG).

**Brand foundations used by every asset**

- **Palette "Court & Flame":** Court Black `#14141A` · Chalk `#F4F1EA` · Flame `#FF4D00` (hero) ·
  Volt `#D8FF3D` (hype) · Grape `#6C4CF1` (night) · Concrete `#8B8B94` (quiet)
- **Type:** Bebas Neue (display, always uppercase) · Hanken Grotesk Black Italic (accent/labels) ·
  Source Sans 3 (body) — Adobe Fonts kit `fvh0euu`
- **Color law:** 60% Chalk/Black · 30% Flame · 10% Volt+Grape
- **Sign-off:** "SEE YOU ON THE FLOOR."

## Consistency rules (apply to all 50)

1. **Clear space:** one cap-height of air around every mark.
2. **60/30/10 color law** on every layout, platform and vendor file.
3. Never stretch/re-track the wordmark; never off-palette colors; never outline or lowercase Bebas;
   never show Kicks without his lace arms.
4. One hook, one CTA, one Flame accent per layout.
5. Every asset ends with the sign-off: **"SEE YOU ON THE FLOOR."**
