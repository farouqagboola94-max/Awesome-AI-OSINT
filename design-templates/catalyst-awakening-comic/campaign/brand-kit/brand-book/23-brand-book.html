01 · MISSION

We are building a Nigerian superhero universe that treats African mythology with the reverence Marvel gave Norse gods — but locates it in the pulse of modern Lagos.

Everything we ship must feel Lagos-first, myth-serious, and street-real. If a piece feels like generic action fantasy, it isn't ours.

05 · VOICE

  • Direct Say the thing. Then stop. "Lagos never sleeps." not "In the city that famously never sleeps".
  • Reverent The Orishas are gods, not props. Never joke about them, never Anglicize their names.
  • Local Lagos street names, coordinates, hours. Specificity is trust.
  • Cinematic Cut like film. Silence is a beat. Whitespace is a shot.
  • Never Fantasy tropes ("realms", "chosen ones"), corporate softening, or cape jokes.
